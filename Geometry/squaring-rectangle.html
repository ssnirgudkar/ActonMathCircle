Things to Discover

Conjecture freely
① What size is the first square?
Try 2×3, 3×4, 4×6, 2×5. For each rectangle, what size is the first square you cut off? Is it always related to the dimensions in a simple way? What happens if you try to cut a square larger than the shorter side?
② The smallest square
Keep cutting until you finish. What is the size of the final square? Try several rectangles — 4×6, 6×9, 3×12. What do you notice about the final square size and the two starting dimensions?
③ Count the squares
How many squares does it take to tile a 1×5? A 1×8? A 2×5? A 3×8? Build a table. Is there a connection between the number of squares and the steps of the Euclidean algorithm?
④ Special rectangles
Which rectangles can be tiled with all equal squares? Try 2×4, 3×6, 4×8, 2×5. What is the condition? How does this connect to GCD being equal to one of the dimensions?
