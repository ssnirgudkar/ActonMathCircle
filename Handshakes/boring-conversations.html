Boring Conversations

Meeples refuse to stand next to the same color twice. Arrange two lineups so no color-pair of neighbors repeats between them.

← All Explorations · Combinatorics

The Rules

Two meeples standing next to each other are having a conversation. Red next to Blue = a Red–Blue conversation. Same color next to same color also counts as a conversation.

Rule: no conversation may happen twice across both lineups. If Red–Blue are neighbors in Lineup 1, they cannot be neighbors in Lineup 2. Find valid arrangements for both!

Things to Discover

① Unique colors first

Set each color count to 1. With 4 meeples of 4 colors, can you always find a valid Lineup 2? How many valid arrangements exist?

② Add repeats

Set Red=2, Blue=2, all others=0. Is it possible? What about Red=3, Blue=1? Which distributions are possible and which are impossible?

③ Circle mode

Switch to circle. Try Red=3, Blue=2, Green=2, Orange=2 (total 9). Can you find a solution?

④ Three lineups

What is the fewest meeples that can meet three times? You'll definitely need more than five colors.
